Time-Series Deep Learning Architectures
Benchmarking different Time-Series Deep Learning architectures with raw CY-BENCH predictors and pinball (MQL) loss for end-of-season Crop-Yield Forecasting Task. All the models are provided with unaggregated daily resolution data which gets encoded over the year to create one sample. These models are evaluated against – A. Error Metrics such as Normalized RMSE, sMAPE, and R², B. Performance against Trend models such as OLS and Mann-kendall, and C. Performance against climateology aggregated via mean of all training data and rolling average of last 3 years.
LPJmL covers all 35 maize + 27 wheat countries, including African maize (AO, BF, ET, LS, ML, MZ, NE, SN, TD, ZA, ZM). Shown as a process-model baseline, not ranked among the architectures.
