TST — Time-Series Transformer & Linear Architectures
Benchmarking Time-Series Transformer (Autoformer, Informer, iTransformer, PatchTST, TimesNet, TimexEr, TSMixer, TST) and Linear (DLinear, NLinear, OLinear, RLinear, XLinear) architectures with raw CY-BENCH predictors and pinball (MQL) loss for end-of-season Crop-Yield Forecasting Task. All models receive unaggregated daily-resolution predictors. Evaluated against Error Metrics (NRMSE, sMAPE, R²), Skill vs Trend baselines, and Skill vs Climatology baselines (mean and rolling-3). Ranking on Skill vs Rolling-3 (hardest baseline). Best-performing models are then forwarded to the Resolution Comparison below.
LPJmL covers all 35 maize + 27 wheat countries, including African maize (AO, BF, ET, LS, ML, MZ, NE, SN, TD, ZA, ZM). Shown as a process-model baseline, not ranked among the architectures.
