F3

Root-zone soil moisture is declining (-4.3 mm) despite stable precipitation — ET₀ is extracting more water

Median RSM shifted -4.32 mm (range -14.96 mm to +14.08 mm); 73% of countries show a decline; 38.7% significant. Precipitation: -0.017 mm/d (37.1% sig). Rising ET₀ is extracting soil moisture faster than precipitation replenishes it, increasing structural agricultural drought risk in the test period.

Methodology for the EDA tab. Numbers generated by scripts/generate_eda.py from data/eda_variable_shift.json and data/eda_peryear.json — re-run to refresh.

1   Train / Test Split

Fixed chronological split per country: last 5 years = test, 2 years before = validation (excluded), all earlier = training.

2   Variable Shift (Results tab)

ColumnDefinition
Shiftmean_test − mean_train in original units, per country
% SigFraction of countries where KS test p < 0.05
% ↑Fraction of countries where test mean > train mean

Annual view: yield = annual value; weather = seasonal median (q50).

3   Extreme Condition Definitions

All thresholds are country-specific percentiles of the training distribution (p10/p90), except heat/frost which use absolute literature-grounded crop-specific values.

ConditionVariableThresholdSource
Extreme Heattmax Days > yield-decline threshold (maize 29°C, wheat 34°C) > p90 of training Schlenker & Roberts 2009 (PNAS); Tack et al. 2015 (PNAS)
Heat Stresstavg Days below GDD base (maize 12°C, wheat 5°C) < p10 of training (fewer cold days = warmer season) Porter & Gawith 1999; standard GDD bases
Cold Stresstavg Days below GDD base > p90 of training Same GDD bases
Frost Risktmin Days below frost threshold (wheat 0°C / maize 2°C) > p90 of training ETCCDI FD index; maize 2°C for canopy-level radiative inversion
Droughtcwb Days with negative CWB (prec − ET₀) > p90 of training CWB as SPEI proxy; Vicente-Serrano et al. 2010
Excess Rainfallprec Days > 50 mm/day and that count > p90 of training ETCCDI R50mm; single threshold (no crop-specific split justified by literature)
Dry Soilrsm Seasonal mean RSM < p10 of training Percentile-based; PWP not in dataset so PAW threshold not used
Waterloggedrsm Seasonal mean RSM > p90 of training Same

4   Yield Impact (Extreme Years tab)

yield_vs_normal = mean yield anomaly in condition years − mean yield anomaly in normal years, both vs the country’s test-period mean (removes structural yield trend to isolate the weather effect).
